This checklist tracks Android-specific completion. The separate native Windows port and its open DOS-compatibility gates are tracked in [WINDOWS_PORT_CHECKLIST.md](WINDOWS_PORT_CHECKLIST.md).

The port is complete only when the compatibility work and Android validation below are closed. A focused regression passing is evidence for that case; it does not establish full runtime parity.
